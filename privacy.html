Privacy Policy

Barber Booking

Last Updated: August 2026

Barber Booking respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and protected when you use the Barber Booking application and website.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect information such as your name, mobile number, email address, profile information, booking details, barber shop information and information required to provide our services.

2. Location Information

Location information may be used when you choose to find nearby barber shops or use location-based features. You can control location permissions through your device settings.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to provide and improve the Barber Booking service, process bookings, communicate booking updates, provide customer support, prevent misuse and maintain service security.

4. Payments

When payment services are introduced, payments may be processed through third-party payment service providers. Barber Booking does not unnecessarily store sensitive payment credentials such as card passwords or UPI PINs.

5. Notifications

If you enable notifications, Barber Booking may send booking updates, confirmations, cancellations and other relevant service notifications.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, misuse or disclosure. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Third-Party Services

Barber Booking may use trusted third-party services for authentication, database, hosting, payment processing, notifications and other necessary functionality. Such services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

8. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain records, resolve disputes, comply with applicable requirements and protect the service.

9. Your Choices

You may control certain permissions through your device settings and may contact us regarding questions about your personal information.

10. Children's Privacy

Barber Booking is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or legal requirements change. Updated versions will be published on this page.

12. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions, please contact Barber Booking through the contact information provided on our website.
